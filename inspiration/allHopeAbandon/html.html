681 AE ( age 0 )
Born the last child of an elderly drakete and an absent avian father. Never knows their siblings growing up on account of the draketes' solitary lifestyle, but is raised unusually close to their mother, who clings to companionship as she dies slowly of old age.
693 AE ( age 12 )
Mother dies, promising to be reunited in the sky. Vows to one day fly to the heavens to see their mother once more.
694 AE ( age 13 )
Tracks down father in the city of Acadia. Father is a high ranking member of a crime organization who runs out of the city. Gets an entry job with the organization as an apprentice mechanic and occasional delivery boy under the alias 'Rook', with the intention of moving up in the group to attract the attention of their father.
699 YEARS AGO ( age 18 )
Meets their father properly for the first time, is immediately dispelled of any fantasy that they can have a normal parent-child relationship. Decides to stay with the group until they save enough money to build their dream airship.
701 AE ( age 20 )
Tasked with sending an invitation to a certain bird who earned the organization's attention. As Eli proves equal parts useful and indifferent to the group, they're tasked with keeping a close eye on his work and come to serve as the official go-between between boss and merc.
702 AE ( age 21 )
Ordered to cut Eli loose on a mission, but instead disobeys orders and nearly gets themselves killed by coming to his rescue.
704 AE ( age 23 )
A sabotaged project results in damage to Rook's eyesight, ruining their dreams of becoming a pilot. After several months of recovery, they are able to return to their mechanic and messenger work, and entrust their dream of flying to Eli.
712 AE ( age 32 )
Diablerie occurs. Binds to Liberty in order to avoid a near-death experience, after which they make for Eli's nearest safe house. Ends up in Astoria after going through empty safe houses, and after reuniting with Eli, settles down with a new workshop to begin research and development into anti-demon weapons and an airship to fly survivors out of Astoria.
